Hero and pet portraits for the kingdom unlock alerts.

Put a picture for each hero or pet in this folder, named like the list below
(.png, .webp or .jpg). A square-ish picture with the face near the top works best.
Anything missing just shows the initials instead.

After adding pictures, rebuild:  docker compose up -d --build
