How it works

The bot does two jobs for your alliance:

  1. Gift codes - every hour it looks for new Kingshot codes and redeems them for every linked account. Nobody has to type codes in.
  2. Reminders - it posts alerts before Bear Trap, Arena reset or any event you add. Members can pick a role to get pinged.

Everything can be done here on the website or with Discord slash commands - use whichever you like.

Good to know

  • Times: forms use UTC, which is Kingshot's game time. Everywhere else the website shows your own time zone.
  • Two Bear Traps: Group 1 and Group 2 are separate reminders with their own roles, so members only get pinged for their group.
  • Defaults: Bear Trap repeats every 48h (alerts 30, 10 and 0 min before). Arena repeats every 24h (60 and 15 min before). Change them to match your alliance.
  • Paused account? Usually the kingdom number is wrong. Edit the player with the right kingdom and it resumes.
  • Auto setup makes a 👑 Kingshot category (gift-codes, bear-trap, arena, events, pick-your-alerts), alert roles and a Kingshot Manager role. Run it again any time - it never duplicates. Give Kingshot Manager to your R4s.
  • Privacy: by default only admins and officers see recent claims and activity. Admins can let members see them, with names and IDs hidden (e.g. 3153•••276).
  • Officers: give a role like R4 access to just the sections they need - view or edit - without Discord admin powers.
  • KvK numbers are typed in by your officers. Kingshot doesn't publish player power, so the comparison is only as good as the list you paste.
  • Colours: tap 🎨 at the top to pick a colour and light/dark mode. It's saved in your browser.
  • Role buttons not working? The bot needs Manage Roles, and its role must sit above the alert roles in Server Settings → Roles.
  • Don't see your server? Click Refresh my servers on the home page.
